# Neurophysiology Formalism for TradePulse

## System Overview

TradePulse orchestrates multiple neurophysiological control loops to stabilise decision making under non-stationary markets. The platform treats each loop as a first-class module with explicit contracts for inputs, state, outputs, and telemetry. The table summarises the primary subsystems and their biological analogies.

| Subsystem | Module path | Physiological analogy | Dominant internal state | Emitted signals |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| Appetitive dopamine loop | `src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py` | Mesolimbic reward prediction and vigour control | `tonic_level`, `phasic_level`, `dopamine_level` | Modulated Q-values, policy temperature, Go/No-Go flags |
| Serotonin stabiliser | `core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py` | Tonic-phasic 5-HT inhibition with meta-adaptation | `tonic_level`, `serotonin_level`, `gate_level`, `sensitivity` | HOLD veto, inhibited action probabilities, gradient tempering |
| Dopamine↔Serotonin action gate | `src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/action_gate.py` | Basal ganglia gating with cortical veto | Cross-checked Go/HOLD/No-Go evaluation | Unified routing decision and exploration temperature |
| Fractal motivation & allostasis | `core/neuro/motivation.py` | Fronto-striatal motivation blend with hypothalamic allostasis | Fractal summaries, bandit posterior, allostatic load | Intrinsic reward, exploration scores, motivation telemetry |
| Fractal energy regulator | `core/neuro/fractal_regulator.py` | Cortico-thalamic energy management and crisis damping | Sliding window of states, last efficiency delta | Hurst/PLE/CSI metrics, energy cost, efficiency delta |
| HPC Active Inference | `neuropro/hpc_active_inference_v4.py` | Hierarchical predictive coding with active inference | Multi-level GRU states, precision weights, actor-critic weights | PWPE-gated actions, blended rewards, perturbation scale |

All controllers expose deterministic APIs and telemetry hooks, enabling formal verification and audit trails.

## Appetitive Dopamine Loop

`DopamineController` implements a TD(0) appetitive pathway. Reward prediction error is computed as \(\delta = r + \gamma V' - V\), persisted in `last_rpe`, and used to update the tabular value estimate via \(V \leftarrow V + \alpha\delta\).【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L318-L352】 Phasic release is modelled as `max(0, δ) * burst_factor`, while tonic level is an exponential moving average of the appetitive drive plus phasic surge, clamped through a logistic transform \(\sigma(k(tonic-\theta))\) to stay within \([0, 1]\).【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L354-L371】 The loop modulates action values multiplicatively (`Q' = Q · (1 + delta_gain · (DA − baseline))`) and adjusts the exploration temperature exponentially with negative-RPE compensation to trigger rapid exploratory bursts.【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L373-L412】 Invigoration and suppression thresholds translate dopamine levels into Go/No-Go flags, enforcing basal ganglia-style vigour gating.【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L420-L437】 Meta-adaptation nudges learning-rate, gain, and base temperature according to drawdown/Sharpe regimes, with cooldown counters preventing oscillations.【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L439-L480】 Telemetry for tonic, phasic, dopamine, and exploration ratio is emitted on the configured cadence, aligning with TACL audit requirements.【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L482-L504】

## Serotonin Stabiliser

`SerotoninController` derives an aversive release signal from weighted volatility, free energy, cumulative losses, and rho-loss complement, guaranteeing non-negative output and optional override weights for experiments.【F:core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py†L162-L205】 Phasic bursts are triggered by a smooth logistic gate over the aversive state, while tonic dynamics follow a decay-calibrated EMA that blends tonic and burst modes. The resulting signal is transformed through \(\sigma(k(tonic-\theta))\) and scaled by an adaptive sensitivity floor (`≥0.1`) enforced by desensitisation counters and exponential decay.【F:core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py†L241-L284】 Action probabilities are inhibited via \(p' = clip(p·(1 − δ·5HT)·(1+za\_bias), 0, 1)\), and HOLD veto activates when serotonin, phasic, or gate channels exceed configured thresholds, optionally subject to a TACL guard function.【F:core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py†L286-L338】 Gradient tempering applies a linear reduction proportional to the serotonin signal to damp exploitation updates.【F:core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py†L340-L356】 Meta-adaptation perturbs release weights using drawdown/Sharpe telemetry while preserving auditability via guarded persistence and mirrored YAML snapshots.【F:core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py†L358-L418】

## Dopamine–Serotonin Action Gate

The `ActionGate` composes dopamine vigour checks with serotonin veto logic. `check_invigoration` and `check_suppress` translate dopamine levels into Go/No-Go decisions, while `check_cooldown` enforces HOLD when serotonin channels trigger veto thresholds. The gate returns a consolidated decision tuple (`go`, `hold`, `no_go`, `temperature`, `dopamine_level`) and respects serotonin-imposed temperature floors to avoid over-exploration during aversive regimes.【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/action_gate.py†L1-L44】【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L420-L437】【F:core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py†L286-L338】

## Fractal Motivation and Allostatic Regulation

`FractalMotivationController` fuses contextual information gain, coherence, and fractal energy into a motivation signal, backed by Thompson-sampling bandits modulated by Hurst-driven scaling.【F:core/neuro/motivation.py†L17-L118】 Intrinsic reward combines value-prediction error, state transition norm, fractal energy, and motivation drive, tempered by an allostatic regulator that integrates reward prediction error and stress via an ODE solved in `AllostaticRegulator.update`.【F:core/neuro/motivation.py†L120-L205】【F:core/neuro/motivation.py†L424-L438】 Unified control scores leverage upper-confidence bounds augmented by motivation-dependent bonuses and hazard-aware penalties, while telemetry captures signal statistics and action entropy for governance dashboards.【F:core/neuro/motivation.py†L206-L335】【F:core/neuro/motivation.py†L440-L492】 The controller guarantees guard-rail compliance by inserting a `pause_and_audit` fallback whenever risk or compliance channels fail.【F:core/neuro/motivation.py†L306-L335】

## Fractal Energy Efficiency Regulator

`EEPFractalRegulator` maintains a sliding state window to compute Hurst exponent, Power Law Exponent, Crisis Stability Index, and embodied energy costs, providing early warning for instability. Metrics are recomputed on each update, with defensive guards for insufficient data and non-finite values. Energy damping is applied through configurable `energy_damping` factors, and efficiency deltas report the regulator’s impact relative to the embodied baseline.【F:core/neuro/fractal_regulator.py†L1-L126】 The regulator exposes structured `RegulatorMetrics` objects to downstream consumers, formalising crisis triggers and efficiency adjustments.【F:core/neuro/fractal_regulator.py†L16-L61】

## Hierarchical Predictive Coding with Active Inference

`HPCActiveInferenceModuleV4` assembles afferent synthesis, multi-level predictive coding, and self-rewarding actor-critic policies. Transformer encoders and GRU-based prediction stacks generate precision-weighted prediction errors, modulated by learnable weights per hierarchy level. The actor produces action logits while the critic and reward predictor enforce self-rewarding updates through blended rewards and learnable perturbation scales. Exploration and exploitation temperatures remain configurable yet positive, aligning with the module’s metastable gating that thresholds precision-weighted errors to trigger regime switches.【F:neuropro/hpc_active_inference_v4.py†L1-L120】

## Integration, Telemetry, and Compliance

All subsystems expose deterministic serialisation via `to_dict` or config dumps, enabling TACL audit trails for state reconstructions.【F:src/tradepulse/core/neuro/dopamine/dopamine_controller.py†L506-L523】【F:core/neuro/serotonin/serotonin_controller.py†L420-L471】 Metrics emitted by dopamine and serotonin controllers carry explicit version tags, while motivation telemetry reports rolling statistics to monitor exploration balance. Coupling the controllers through `ActionGate` ensures appetitive invigoration never bypasses aversive veto channels, preserving compliance with the platform’s HOLD-first doctrine. When deploying new strategies, instrument the pipeline to persist regulator metrics and motivation telemetry alongside trading decisions to evidence neurophysiological guard rails during audits.
