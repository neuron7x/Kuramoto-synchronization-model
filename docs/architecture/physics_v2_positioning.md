# Physics v2 Positioning — Verification-First Market Physics Coordinate

Status: **draft positioning contract**  
Parent PR: **#1284 — Physics v2 universal synchronization manifold architecture**  
Scope: define the unique market coordinate GeoSync occupies before implementation work expands.

## 1. Positioning statement

GeoSync Physics v2 is a verification-first research infrastructure for testing whether market microstructure can be represented as a time-indexed causal graph whose curvature, synchronization, and falsification behavior expose regime structure inside declared data domains.

It is not a trading product, signal vendor, universal market theory, or visual analytics dashboard.

The coordinate is:

```text
market microstructure
+ geometric physics
+ synchronization dynamics
+ machine-checkable law witnesses
+ provenance capsules
= falsifiable market-physics research substrate
```

## 2. Unique space

GeoSync does not compete as another alpha model. It occupies the space between:

1. **Quant research platforms** — strong on backtests, weak on physical contracts.
2. **Graph ML systems** — strong on embeddings, weak on falsifiable law boundaries.
3. **Market microstructure studies** — strong on empirical detail, weak on reusable verification infrastructure.
4. **Physics-inspired finance notebooks** — often expressive, rarely machine-checkable.

GeoSync's unique claim is narrower and harder:

> A market-physics claim is accepted only when the same chain contains a validity domain, a witness, a falsifier, and a reproducibility capsule.

That is the product-coordinate, not the marketing phrase.

## 3. What nobody else solves the same way

The intended gap is not "predict price better". That is surface competition.

The intended gap is:

```text
How do we make a geometry-of-markets claim reviewable, falsifiable, reproducible, and bounded by data-domain evidence?
```

GeoSync answers this by forcing every positive statement through:

```text
L2/order-flow source
-> causal graph snapshot
-> curvature/synchronization frame
-> law witness
-> negative controls
-> evidence capsule
-> claim maturity label
```

The system's value is not the beauty of Ricci or Kuramoto terms. The value is that claims cannot enter the repo as doctrine unless their failure modes are also present.

## 4. Strategic boundary

Allowed:

- discrete graph curvature over declared market graphs;
- Kuramoto-family synchronization descriptors;
- causal-cutoff constraints inspired by finite information propagation;
- deterministic synthetic fixtures;
- real L2 claims only with licensed manifests and replay artifacts;
- warn-level draft laws before blocking enforcement.

Not allowed:

- predictive alpha claims without out-of-sample falsification;
- production trading readiness;
- universal market physics;
- relativity equivalence language;
- silent fallback from real data to synthetic data;
- law promotion without test-bound witnesses.

## 5. Market coordinate

The clean external sentence is:

> GeoSync is a verification-first market-physics research substrate that tests geometric and synchronization hypotheses on market microstructure with machine-checkable laws, falsifiers, and reproducibility capsules.

The engineering sentence is:

> GeoSync turns Ricci/Kuramoto/fractal market hypotheses into typed contracts and evidence artifacts that can be reviewed, replayed, weakened, or rejected.

The anti-positioning sentence is:

> GeoSync is not an alpha engine; it is a claim-validation engine for physics-inspired market structure.

## 6. Success condition

Physics v2 is positioned correctly when a reviewer can answer all of the following without reading private context:

1. What exact market object is being modeled?
2. What physical analogy is being used?
3. Where does the analogy stop?
4. Which law witnesses are machine-checked?
5. Which falsifiers were attempted?
6. Which dataset and config produced the result?
7. What claim maturity level is allowed?

If any answer is missing, the positioning has collapsed into branding noise.

## 7. Fixed invariant

```text
GeoSync must be positioned as a falsifiable verification substrate, never as a guaranteed predictive market engine.
```
